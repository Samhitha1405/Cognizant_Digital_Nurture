Community Bulletin

Technova 2026 introduces exciting workshops, coding hackathons, innovation expos, leadership activities, dance competitions, and cultural celebrations exclusively for women students. Participants from different colleges will engage in creative competitions, technical sessions, networking activities, and inspiring events. The fest aims to empower women in technology, leadership, and innovation through collaborative learning and vibrant community experiences.
